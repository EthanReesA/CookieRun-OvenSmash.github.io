IMPACT

The impact of Cookie Run: OvenSmash shows how game can fall with low quailty updates and bad gameplay.
A report from ChosunBiz explains that alot of issues is tied to technical polish surfaced from the start. Complaints spread quickly about the overall quality problems such as loading delays, stutter, and character balance.
They say it's curcial for the game to have real-time battles, network stability, controls, and the overview of the field, but the expectations fell short in these areas.
However, this game is new and has just been lanuched a month ago. So, it might get better soon hopefully.

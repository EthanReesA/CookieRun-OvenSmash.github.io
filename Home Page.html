ABOUT

Platter City

Step into a metropolis of sweet wishes and thrilling energy! Welcome to Platter City, where the towers of sugary goodness reach for the clouds, glittery digital billboards illumunate the streets, and mesmerising holograms captivate at every turn! The metropolis pulses with energy as Cookies from different clans and factions of many flavors that flaunt their unique character. Behind Platter City stands the "W.I.T.C.H Holdings," the corporate powerhouse that orchestrates the famous Oven Crown Cup. But questions remain under the shimmering city, what's the true source of their boundless wealth and technological mastery?






DJ Miya

The ambassador of the lovely, breathtaking Platter city, the star of every Cookie's eye, It's DJ Miya! Rumours has it, that she made a wish to make everyone love her after winning the last season's Oven Crown Cup and with her rising music selection, sweet talking skills, and irresistible charm... No Cookie will ever resist her cute and sweet charm! Her voice always reaches every single corner of Platter City without a doubt!






Oven Crown Cup

Oven Crowns appear at the peaceful city and chaos erupts within! However, don't be alarmed as Cookies who seize the most crowns will have their deepest wishes fulfilled. Driven with their compelling backstories and burning desires, every Cookie clash in the intense Oven Crown Cup, for battles of fate and destiny commence!
